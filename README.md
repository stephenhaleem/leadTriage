# Lead Navigator

A marketing agency receives hundreds of inbound leads every month. Each lead includes contact details, company information, and notes from previous conversations. Currently, the team manually reviews these leads to determine who is worth contacting, who needs nurturing, and who is unlikely to be a good fit.




The goal of this project is to build an automated lead qualification system that can process a lead export, assess intent and fit based on the available data, assign a score to each lead, and prioritise the best opportunities.

You will need to clean the data, analyse the lead notes, define your own qualification criteria, and produce a ranked list of leads with a recommendation for whether they should be contacted now, nurtured, or disqualified.

The solution should be reusable so it can be run against future lead exports with minimal manual effort.
